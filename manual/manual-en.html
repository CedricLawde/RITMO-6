THE FRONT PANEL

1
Mixer — level slider, PAN, TRIG (hear the voice), MUTE (takes the voice out of the Mix; its separate output keeps playing), for each voice A to F.
2
16-step × 6-voice grid — click or drag to set/clear steps. The lit column follows playback.
3
PAD MODE (v2) — what a click on a step does: NOTE on / off · MULTI 1 to 4 hits, lit green · ACCENT louder (red bar, ACCENT knob) · PROB 100, 75, 50 or 25 % chance · LENGTH end of the voice's own loop (display at the end of the row).
4
ONE / ALL — ONE plays only that row (solo), ALL brings every row back.
5
PROGRAM — 16 patterns. STEPS — pattern length, 1 to 16 steps.
6
RESET · COPY · PASTE · RANDOM — clear, copy, paste or randomize the current pattern.
7
TEMPO 1–10 in INTER; in MIDI it follows your DAW and becomes a divider (1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/32).
8
STOP / START (space bar in the standalone) and KEYB CTRL: pick programs and play from a MIDI keyboard.
9
SOUND — MINI: the punchy Minipops-style sound; ORIG: the model of the original plugin.
10
KIT — MINI + 16 factory kits (◀ ▶). LOAD / SAVE: the whole bank (patterns, sounds, effects).
11
MIDI CHANNEL — All or channel 1 to 16.
12
Modules A01 to F06 — click to open the sound editor. TUNE with its note (click = exact tuning), then the 4 small knobs PH · DS · DL · SP send the voice to the effects.
13
TEMPO DRIFT — 50 %: steady; towards 0 % steps randomly drag behind, towards 100 % they rush ahead. SOUND DRIFT — every hit sounds a little different. SWING — 50 % (straight) to 75 %: every second 16th falls late.
14
NUDGE — moves a voice up to ½ step early or late (claps just before the snare: the disco groove). DRIFT — how much of SOUND DRIFT this voice gets.
15
FX — the front plate turns over on its hinge and shows the effects panel on its back.
⚙
SETUP (v2.1) — window size 60 · 80 · 100 · 130 · 160 %; in the standalone, Audio output (WASAPI or ASIO) and MIDI inputs; About: website, updates.
16
CHAIN (v2.2) — the RITMO·6 plate turns over to show the pattern chain: 16 slots (click or wheel = a program, right-click = empty), played up to the first empty slot, then looped. ON / OFF · RANDOM random order · TAKE YOUR PICK 2 to 16 patterns at random, no gap.

THE SOUND MODULE

TONE OSCTUNE pitch (its note shows on the right; click it to tune exactly) · DECAY length · VOLUME · P.DECAY / P.DEPTH pitch drop (the "pew" of toms and kicks) · PM RATE / PM LEV shape and metallic edge.

NOISEnoise VOLUME · DECAY · filter FREQ · RESO · F.DECAY · LP / HP switch.

PAN RNDrandom panning on every hit.

RANDrandom sound · LOAD / SAVEone sound · TRIG · CLOSE

Knobs: drag up/down · Shift = fine · mouse wheel · double-click = default. Hover to read the value.

THE FX PANEL

4-stage PHASER (RATE, DEPTH, COLOR) · op-amp DISTORTION (DRIVE, FILTER, FAT) · DELAY in TAPE, BBD or MOD mode, SYNC (note values) or FREE (ms) · 3-spring SPRING REVERB, with KICK to knock the tank.
Each effect has its own BYPASS and level. Turn up each voice's PH DS DL SP sends (12); click FX to go back to the front.

MIDI KEYBOARD

36–41 · voices A–F
42–57 · programs 1–16
58 and up · hold = play
Programs and play: KEYB CTRL on.

INSTALLATION

Copy Ritmo-6.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, then rescan plugins in your DAW: it shows up under the vendor Geranium.
Also: Ritmo-6.clap and the standalone app Ritmo-6.exe. 6 separate outputs A–F (dry, no effects) + stereo mix with effects. Window size: SETUP menu (⚙).
